President's Message
Senshi Goju-Ryu Karate Championship 2026

It is my great pleasure and honour to welcome all karatekas, coaches, instructors, officials, parents and supporters to the Senshi Goju-Ryu Karate Championship 2026.

This championship is more than a competition. It is an opportunity for our young karatekas to demonstrate their discipline, courage, respect, sportsmanship and dedication to the martial art of Karate.

I would like to express my sincere appreciation to all participating clubs and dojos, coaches, referees, officials, volunteers and parents who have contributed their time, effort and support towards making this championship possible.

A special and heartfelt thank you goes to all our sponsors and generous supporters. Your valuable contributions and commitment have played an important role in making this championship a reality. We truly appreciate your trust and support for the development of Karate and our young athletes.

To all competitors, give your very best on the tatami. Win with humility, accept every result with dignity, and always respect your opponents and officials. Remember that true success is not only measured by medals, but also by the character, discipline and experience gained through competition.

On behalf of Kelab Karate Do Senshi Goju-Ryu, I wish everyone a safe, successful and memorable championship.

OSU!
